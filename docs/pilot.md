# Pilot validation

A *Voltes V* episode 1 pilot covered two selected dialogue scenes, **2 minutes 27 seconds**, with **31 complete performances across 11 character identities**.

The export is 640×480, about 9.4 MB, with one Bahasa Indonesia audio track. Original music and effects come from Bandit v2 separation. No songs were dubbed. The source's burned English captions and watermark were preserved.

The pilot used Scribe v2 plus independent Japanese ASR, scene evidence, direct adaptation, editorial review, Gemini 3.8 Flash TTS, measured fitting, selective retakes, M&E mixing and full decode/stream verification. A model listening check flagged a rushed warning; the line was condensed and rerecorded. Old takes and receipts were retained. Maximum measured pitch-preserving speedup was about 1.081×.

This pilot exercised serial dialogue, casting, separation, retakes and single-track delivery. It did not test full-episode coverage, simultaneous dialogue or precise lip synchronization. Each production still needs listening review for adaptation, pronunciation, acting and separation quality. Synthetic tests exercise mechanics separately; replaying the pilot's saved source evidence, stems and recordings checks the reusable tools without new paid requests.

Episode media, dialogue, generated voices, subtitles and provider receipts are excluded from the repository.
