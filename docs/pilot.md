# Demonstrated pilot

A local *Voltes V* episode 1 audition established the preferred workflow on 2026-10-28. It contains two selected dialogue scenes, **2 minutes 27 seconds**, with **31 complete performances across 11 character identities**. The requester auditioned it and described the result as very good, then requested Indonesian-only delivery.

The accepted export is 640×480, about 9.4 MB, with one Bahasa Indonesia audio track. Original music and effects come from actual Bandit v2 separation. No songs were dubbed. The supplied source's English captions and watermark were already burned into its picture and were preserved.

The pilot used Scribe v2 plus independent Japanese ASR, scene evidence, direct adaptation, agent review, Gemini 3.8 Flash TTS, measured fitting, selective retakes, M&E mixing and full decode/stream verification. A model listening check flagged a rushed warning; the line was condensed and rerecorded before the final audition. Old takes/receipts were retained. Maximum measured pitch-preserving speedup was about 1.081×.

This establishes a successful short audition, not a full-episode benchmark or native-speaker certification. Lip timing is approximate; future casts and separation need listening. Synthetic tests exercise mechanics separately. The reusable tools are also replayed against the local pilot's saved source evidence, stems and accepted recordings without new paid requests.

No video, screenshot, dialogue, generated voice, subtitle file, local path or receipt from that episode is published here. The pilot files remain on the local machine.
